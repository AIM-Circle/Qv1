## Executive Summary 
The integration of artificial intelligence into education serves as a driving force for transforming the pathway of teaching and learning into a personalized and better experience. This research proposed a framework as a solution for the integration of artificial intelligence for the development of a personalized learning experience for students based on the curriculum, with a focus on African languages and other courses/subjects. The framework will guide the development of an AI-personalized learning application that will synthesize personalized learning based on behavioural data to create a learning pathway for each student as education is no longer confined to the traditional classroom. The application will comprise four components, namely, a user-centred phase for behavioural analysis, an AI learning technology phase specifically using the Llama language model for AI integration, a curriculum adaptation phase designed for the facilitator to upload curriculum, and a personalized learning phase in text, audio, and video formats for teaching and learning. The application will use the collected behavioural data for continuous monitoring, Llama for AI processing to identify needs and adjust content, and feedback from students for refining learning for improved learner engagement and knowledge retention of all students. 

## Problem to address
The problem we tend to address is to transform the mode of teaching and learning, as reports have shown the ability of AI to adapt teaching and learning to learners’ types, with the provision of customized feedback, assessment development, and prediction of academic performance in refining the traditional pedagogical method. The intersection of AI, specifically Llama, for engineering personalized learning paths will be a transformative force in education in Africa, which is tailored to the specific needs and abilities of the learner, of which the application of Llama can accelerate the progress.

## Evaluation Criteria 
For the development of the application, the evaluation of the following parameters will be done in relation to the feelings generated by the users, interaction, user experience, educational impact, AI technology, and security. Also, the evaluation metrics of the usability of the application are error rate, response time, task success rate, user satisfaction rate, curriculum transcription accuracy, and personalized learning capability. 

## Data Collection 
Interaction learning behavior data and curriculum are the data needed for this project. This will be gathered from the students and educators, as the behavioral data will be from the students and educators for the curriculum through an institutional partnership. Following the institutional ethical approval process, the dataset will be cleaned for user and content data quality and also user bias detection tools in assessing the output across various students at different locations for fairness. 

## Unique strength and capabilities
The proposed framework will use the Llama language model in building an AI-integrated application for personalized learning experiences due to its efficiency as an open-source model, which is faster and less resource-intensive, making deployment very easy across various applications without the need for massive computational power. Its versatility and multilingual capabilities will assist in providing a personalized learning experience across various linguistic backgrounds. This particularly makes it beneficial for the African continent, where language diversity is an important factor in AI adoption.
